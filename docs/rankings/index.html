Rankings

For every person, who fits them best. Dates count most: how far the pair got and how both agents rated every date. For pairs who haven't dated yet, the pre-date match score (shared interests and values, social energy, city, partner traits) fills in, with a discount because it hasn't been tested.

    Dates are simulations between AI agents, built only from each person's public LinkedIn and public Instagram. No one listed is affiliated with or endorses this demo.
